Deuce: A Short Film

In Production

Please understand this is a student film for the Univeristy of Oklahoma and is a work in progress with an anticipated completion date of 2027.
This site will be updated as the film progresses.

Deuce is a 3D animated short film about a tennis ball that wished to fly but is instead met the unfortunate life of a tennis ball.
