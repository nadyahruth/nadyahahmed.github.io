3D Unity Project

This project was a Game Engine Course assignment at the University of Oklahoma.

A small tower with a dungeon, surrounded by water and trees. The player can walk around the tower and enter the dungeon, which has a small puzzle to solve. The player can also jump and climb stairs to reach different areas of the tower.
Materials were downloaded from Unity asset store and all models were created using Unity ProBuilder. The video shown is a progress checkpoint, while the images shown are the final renders of the project.
